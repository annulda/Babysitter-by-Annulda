Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Some details are best discussed before confirming a booking.

Do you accept newborn bookings?

Yes, newborns are welcome. Please share the baby's routine and care instructions in advance so we can discuss the arrangement.

How do I confirm a booking?

Send an enquiry through the booking form or WhatsApp. The date, schedule, location, rate and any special arrangements must be agreed before the booking is confirmed.

Can you travel outside Kuala Lumpur or overseas?

Travel may be discussed in advance and is subject to availability, agreed fees, travel arrangements and any applicable entry or work requirements.

What should parents prepare?

Please share the child's schedule, emergency contact details, feeding and sleep routine, allergies or relevant care instructions directly and securely before the session.
